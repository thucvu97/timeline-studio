# Stock Footage Integrations

## Task Description

Integration of major stock footage platforms (Shutterstock, Unsplash, Pexels, Epidemic Sound) directly into Timeline Studio, enabling seamless search, preview, and licensing of professional media assets.

## Goals

1. **Seamless Integration** - search and import without leaving the app
2. **Multiple Providers** - support for major stock platforms
3. **Smart Search** - AI-powered content discovery
4. **License Management** - automatic tracking and compliance
5. **Offline Cache** - smart caching for performance

## Supported Platforms

### Video & Photo
- **Shutterstock** - premium stock footage and images
- **Unsplash** - free high-quality photos
- **Pexels** - free stock videos and photos
- **Pixabay** - free media assets

### Audio
- **Epidemic Sound** - royalty-free music
- **Artlist** - music and SFX
- **AudioJungle** - stock audio marketplace

## Key Features

### Search & Discovery
- Unified search across all platforms

- Mood and style filters
- Trending content

### Preview & Import
